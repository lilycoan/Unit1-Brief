    How these numbers are made

    Per-prompt energy, carbon, and water figures come from the open-source EcoLogits library (v0.10). Carbon is priced on the electricity grid of the region you compare against, plus EcoLogits' embodied hardware emissions. Water is EcoLogits' blue-water (freshwater actually consumed) figure. Training is excluded — split across all users it adds very little to any one person's footprint.

    Everyday and yearly comparison figures (diet, driving, flights, home energy, coffee, flights, lawns, and so on) are drawn from EPA, EIA, Ember, Our World in Data, Poore & Nemecek (2018), Wynes & Nicholas (2017), the Founders Pledge Climate & Lifestyle report, and the Water Footprint Network.
