## Client need and project challenge

Now that your development environment is configured and working, its time to move into phase 2. The client is a national creative-media company introducing generative AI into its design, writing, production, and software-development workflows. It needs a calculator that represents employees' professional AI use and places its estimated resource use in context alongside digital habits and other everyday activities. The calculator must serve employees with different roles, experiences, and concerns, helping them reach informed conclusions through clearly presented evidence, methodology, limitations, and uncertainty.

Your challenge is to research those needs and deliver an expanded calculator that meets them. Remember, the goal is not to convince employees that AI is harmless or harmful.

## Project requirements

- Add five meaningful features. Each must change what an employee can learn or do and contribute to the final calculation; styling or wording changes do not count.
- At least two features must improve how the calculator represents professional AI use, and at least two must connect AI use to employees' broader digital lives.
- The fifth feature must address the strongest remaining need identified through research.
- Together, the features must serve all three reference employee profiles in `research.md`.
- Cite every factual or numerical claim, explain the methodology, and communicate important limitations and uncertainty so estimates are not presented as certain facts. Follow the original calculator's example.
- The completed calculator must work reliably, run locally, and match the approved technical specification.
